Carpet Water Damage Restoration in Fort Walton Beach, FL

Wet carpet and padding hold a lot of water. Quick extraction and drying can save carpet from clean water, while contaminated water usually means replacement.

Call (844) 950-6900
24/7 dispatch line open

Carpet looks like the top layer of the problem, but the padding and subfloor below take on the most water. Our dispatch line is open 24/7. Stay off wet carpet to avoid pushing water deeper, lift furniture legs onto blocks and remove small items.

Can your carpet be saved?

It depends on the water category and how long the carpet stayed wet. Clean water caught early often lets crews save carpet and replace only the pad. Gray water is borderline. Contaminated water from a flood or sewer means the carpet and padding go.

Water sourceCategoryCarpet outcome
Supply line, broken pipe1 (clean)Often saved if dried within a day or two.
Washing machine, dishwasher2 (gray)Case by case.
Toilet overflow, sewer, flood3 (black)Replace carpet and pad.
Old leak, long standingDegradedUsually replace.

Categories follow ANSI/IICRC S500.

Carpet restoration process

  1. Extract water

    Truck-mounted or portable extractors pull water from carpet and pad. See emergency water extraction.

  2. Decide on the pad

    Padding usually comes out and is replaced. It holds water and cannot be dried reliably.

  3. Dry the carpet and subfloor

    Carpet is lifted and air movers dry underneath. Dehumidifiers support the process.

  4. Clean and sanitize

    Saved carpet is cleaned and treated. Tack strips are checked for corrosion.

What to do while you wait

  • Do not walk on wet carpet more than necessary.
  • Do not use a household vacuum on wet carpet.
  • Place foil or blocks under furniture legs to prevent stains.
  • Pull up the edge in a corner only if you are comfortable doing so, to check the pad.
  • Photograph the area and the source for your records.
  • Keep pets and children off the wet area.

Pad, tack strips and subfloor: the hidden layers

The visible carpet is only one layer. Under it sit padding, tack strips and a subfloor. Padding soaks up the most water and is normally replaced. Tack strips can corrode if left wet. The subfloor, whether concrete or plywood, has to be dry before new padding goes down.

LayerWhat happens when wetUsual action
Carpet faceHolds water, can shrink or stain.Extract, dry, clean or replace.
PaddingActs like a sponge.Remove and replace.
Tack stripCan rust or loosen.Inspect and replace if damaged.
SubfloorHolds moisture and can swell or stay damp.Dry and verify with a meter.

Drying the subfloor is what keeps a damp smell from coming back.

Stairs, area rugs and wall-to-wall carpet

Wall-to-wall carpet is usually lifted at a corner and dried with air movers underneath. Area rugs are easier. They can be lifted, dried flat and cleaned or replaced. Stair carpet is harder, because water runs down the treads and pools in the room below. Crews often treat the bottom of the stairs and the floor beneath as one wet zone.

What affects the cost of carpet water damage restoration

  • Square footage and number of rooms.
  • Water category and time wet.
  • Carpet and pad quality.
  • Subfloor condition.
  • Drying time and equipment.
  • Replacement carpet, if needed.

Carpet over slab and carpet on stairs

On a slab, water spreads laterally under the carpet and into baseboards. On stairs, water runs down and pools in lower rooms. Drying crews check both. For wall damage next to wet carpet, see wall and drywall water damage repair. For hard floors, read about hardwood floor repair.

Insurance note for carpet

Carpet replacement may be covered when water damage is covered. Depreciation rules vary. Ask your insurer, and keep samples and photos.

Cleaning and deodorizing saved carpet

Carpet that is saved is cleaned with hot water extraction after drying. Deodorizing follows if needed. The goal is to remove residue and prevent odor. Ask for readings that show the carpet and the subfloor are both dry before the furniture goes back.

Questions we hear from Fort Walton Beach callers

Can wet carpet be dried instead of replaced?

Yes if the water was clean and drying began soon. The pad is usually replaced.

How long can carpet stay wet before it must be removed?

The sooner the better. Water that stays more than a day or two raises the risk of odor and mold.

Do you handle carpet water emergencies at night or on weekends?

The dispatch line is open 24/7. Call any hour.

Will the carpet shrink?

It can if dried improperly. Crews use controlled airflow to limit shrinkage.

What about the smell?

Odor often means moisture remains. Further drying and cleaning address it.

Can the pad be reused?

Almost never. Pad holds water and breaks down, so it is replaced in most jobs.
