About Fort Walton Beach Water Damage Pros

We run the dispatch line for water damage in Fort Walton Beach and nearby towns. Here is how it works and what we do and do not do.

Call (844) 950-6900

What this service is

Fort Walton Beach Water Damage Pros is a 24/7 dispatch line for water damage. When you call, a dispatcher gathers the facts and connects you with licensed and insured restoration professionals who serve Fort Walton Beach, Mary Esther, Shalimar, Valparaiso and Destin. The goal is simple. Get the right crew to the right job without a long search while water is spreading.

How a call works

  1. You call

    The dispatcher asks where the water came from and whether it is safe to stay.

  2. Safety steps first

    You hear what to do while you wait, such as closing a valve or staying out of water near outlets.

  3. A crew is arranged

    Same-day response is offered when crews are available.

  4. Plan before work

    The crew explains the scope and price before work starts.

Need help now? Call the dispatch line.

Standards we point to

Restoration professionals commonly work to ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for water damage restoration. It covers water categories, classes, equipment placement and drying goals. You can ask any company whether its crew follows it. We do not claim firm certifications on behalf of any contractor.

What we do not do

  • We do not repair plumbing, roofs or electrical systems.
  • We do not install waterproofing or sump systems.
  • We do not give medical advice. Health questions belong to a physician.
  • We do not rent drying equipment for do-it-yourself setups.
  • We do not promise insurance outcomes.

Questions people ask before they call

Is this a restoration company?

It is a dispatch line. A dispatcher listens, gives safety steps and arranges licensed and insured restoration professionals for the job.

Who shows up at my house?

A crew from the professionals who serve your area. They explain who they are and what they plan to do before work starts.

Do you quote prices on the phone?

No. Scope and price are explained after the crew measures what is wet, because a guess helps nobody.

Do you work with my insurance?

The crew documents the loss with photos, readings and a written scope. Coverage decisions stay with your insurer.

Safety guidance every caller hears

Water damage is also a safety event. Every caller hears the same short list before anything else. Stop the source if it is safe. Stay out of water that touches electrical items. Do not enter water that may be sewage or floodwater. Leave the house and call 911 or the utility if you smell gas or see sparks.

Those steps are not a sales script. They come from the same safety principles the industry standard describes, and they apply whether or not you hire anyone.

Our commitments in plain words

  • We do not make health claims about mold or water. Questions about health go to a physician.
