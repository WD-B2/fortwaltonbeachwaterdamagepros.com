Kitchen Water Damage Restoration in Fort Walton Beach, FL

Kitchen leaks soak cabinets, subfloors and walls behind fixtures. Fast drying can save solid materials and limit what has to be replaced.

Call (844) 950-6900
24/7 dispatch line open

Kitchens hold more water sources than any other room. Sinks, supply lines, dishwashers, refrigerators and ice makers can all leak. Our dispatch line is open 24/7. Close the shutoff valve under the sink or behind the appliance and clear the cabinet base so the crew can see the damage.

Check these places first

  • Under-sink supply lines and drain traps for drips or corrosion.
  • The cabinet base for swelling, stains or a soft floor.
  • Behind the dishwasher and refrigerator for wet flooring.
  • Countertop seams near the sink for loose caulk.
  • Walls beside the sink for bubbling paint or discoloration.
  • The floor around the base of cabinets for lifting laminate or vinyl.

What a kitchen leak does to cabinets and floors

Particleboard cabinets swell and fall apart when wet. Plywood boxes hold up better but can still delaminate. Under the cabinets, subfloor absorbs water and stays wet for days. Vinyl or laminate flooring traps moisture beneath it, so the surface can look fine while the floor underneath is soaked.

Restoration process for kitchens

  1. Find the source

    A meter and a thermal camera show the path of the water behind and under cabinets.

  2. Remove standing water and open up

    Toe kicks, baseboards and drawers come off. Wet flooring is cut out if it cannot dry.

  3. Dry cabinets and subfloor

    Mats, injection drying and dehumidifiers dry hidden areas. See structural drying.

  4. Hand off to the rebuild

    Cabinets, countertops and flooring are repaired or replaced after readings are dry.

What else the water touches in a kitchen

  • Countertops: stone and laminate are fine on top, but the particleboard under laminate can swell.
  • Backsplash and wall tile: grout and adhesive can let water behind the wall.
  • Dishwasher and refrigerator: water can run under both and into the floor.
  • Toe kicks and cabinet bottoms: first places to swell.
  • Drawer boxes and shelves: keep them out of the wet zone.

What affects the cost of kitchen water damage work

  • Water category and duration of the leak.
  • Cabinet materials and how much must be removed.
  • Flooring type, including tile, vinyl, laminate or hardwood.
  • Whether water reached adjoining rooms.
  • Drying days.
  • Rebuild: cabinets, counters, flooring and paint.

When the leak is a fixture, not a pipe

Many kitchen losses start with a small fixture. A loose drain trap or a cracked supply hose drips for weeks before a cabinet swells. Replace old rubber hoses with braided lines and check under the sink monthly. If a refrigerator line is the cause, see appliance leak damage. If the floor is hardwood, see hardwood floor water damage repair.

Insurance note for kitchens

Sudden leaks are often covered. Slow drips found after cabinets swell are often questioned. Take photos, keep the failed part and ask your insurer what applies.

Protect the kitchen going forward

  • Replace rubber supply hoses with braided stainless lines.
  • Check under the sink monthly for drips and rust.
  • Know where the sink shutoff valves are.
  • Do not run the dishwasher when you leave for days.
  • Install a small leak sensor under the sink.

When to call right away

  • Water is spreading across the floor or under the cabinets.
  • A cabinet base is swelling or the floor feels soft.
  • You cannot find or close the shutoff valve.
  • Water is near outlets or the refrigerator's electrical connection. Do not touch them.

Crews dry kitchens following the ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage standard and track progress with moisture readings.

Questions we hear from Fort Walton Beach callers

Can kitchen cabinets be dried and saved?

Solid wood and plywood often can. Particleboard that swells is usually replaced.

Do I need to remove the countertop?

Sometimes. If water is trapped under the counter or cabinets, removal can speed drying.

Do you handle kitchen water emergencies at night?

The dispatch line is open 24/7. A sink or dishwasher leak can be urgent, so call any hour.

How long does it take to dry a kitchen?

Often several days, depending on materials and humidity.

Will I need a plumber too?

If a pipe or fitting failed, yes. A plumber repairs it, and the crew dries the damage.

What about the dishwasher?

Pull it out to check the floor. Water often runs under it and into the subfloor.
