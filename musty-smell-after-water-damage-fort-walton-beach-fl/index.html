Musty Smell After Water Damage in Fort Walton Beach, FL

A musty smell after a leak usually means moisture is still hiding somewhere. Find the wet material and the smell has a reason to go away.

Call (844) 950-6900

By the Fort Walton Beach Water Damage Pros team

The musty odor that follows a leak is a clue, not just a nuisance. It means something is still wet or recently was. Air fresheners cover the smell without fixing the cause. This guide helps you find where the smell comes from and what to do about it.

What the smell usually tells you

A musty smell can come from damp insulation, wet drywall, carpet pad, subfloor or a closed-in cavity. In humid coastal air, small amounts of trapped moisture linger for a long time. The smell may be stronger in warm, closed rooms and in the first hours after the AC starts.

Where to look first

  • Behind baseboards and under the edge of carpet.
  • Inside cabinet bases under sinks.
  • Closets that share a wall with a bathroom or utility room.
  • Around the air handler closet and return vents.
  • Attic spaces below roof leaks.
  • Crawl spaces and garages.

A simple sorting guide

ClueLikely sourceNext step
Smell near a wall with stainsWet drywall or insulation.Moisture check, open the wall if needed.
Smell near carpet edgeWet padLift a corner, extract and dry.
Smell when AC runsWet duct, drain pan or air handler area.HVAC check and drying.
Smell in a closed room after rainRoof or window leak.Find and fix the entry.

A moisture meter is more reliable than your nose.

What to do and what not to do

  • Do find and stop any active leak.
  • Do dry the area with a dehumidifier and airflow.
  • Do call for a moisture check if the smell lasts more than a day or two.
  • Do not spray fragrance or bleach over the problem.
  • Do not paint over damp surfaces.
  • Do not make health claims about the smell. Ask a physician about health concerns.

Mold, drying and the standard

Mold needs moisture to grow. Drying removes that moisture. If you can see growth, the cleanup is a mold remediation job, and the ANSI/IICRC S520 standard covers it. Ask whether your state licenses mold work before you hire. Drying alone does not guarantee mold will not return. For drying details, see structural drying, and for wall issues, see wall and drywall repair.

Prevent the smell next time

  • Fix leaks fast.
  • Run the AC and a dehumidifier after a wet event.
  • Keep gutters and drains clear.
  • Do not leave wet towels, boxes or carpet in closed rooms.
  • Inspect under sinks twice a year.

A step-by-step way to track the smell

  1. Rule out the obvious

    Check trash, drains and the refrigerator. Not every odor comes from water damage.

  2. Close the room for an hour

    The smell will concentrate and help you locate the area.

  3. Check surfaces with your hand

    Cold, damp or soft spots on walls and floors are leads.

  4. Use a meter

    A moisture meter confirms what your nose suggests.

  5. Open the hidden spot if needed

    Remove a baseboard or a small piece of drywall to see inside.

Odor products: when they help and when they do not

Deodorizing products can help after the source is dried and cleaned. Used before drying, they cover the smell and hide the clue. Hydroxyl and ozone machines are tools professionals use in unoccupied spaces for odor control, and they come after the cleanup is complete. Ask what a company plans to use and why.

When to call for help

Call if the smell lasts more than a day or two after a leak, if a wall or ceiling stain grows, or if you see visible growth. A professional can check hidden moisture, explain the findings and tell you whether drying or remediation is the next step. You do not need to guess.

Cleanup after the cause is fixed

  • Dry the area until meters read normal.
  • Remove materials that stayed wet.
  • Clean hard surfaces.
  • Replace filters and clean vents if the smell reached the HVAC.
  • Re-check in a few weeks.

Local note: storms far away still matter

Hurricane Sally came ashore near Gulf Shores, Alabama, as a Category 2 storm on September 16, 2020, and the Associated Press photographed waves hitting a dock near the Brooks Bridge in Fort Walton Beach the day before. The point is that a storm does not have to land here to push wind-driven rain and water toward local homes. After any such storm, check for odors, stains and damp corners within a few days.

Questions readers ask

Does a musty smell always mean mold?

No, but it means moisture. A check can tell what is behind the smell.

Will the smell go away by itself?

It may fade, but the moisture often remains. Dry the area.

Can I use a dehumidifier alone?

It helps, but wet wall cavities and pad may need professional drying.

Should I move out because of the smell?

That is a health question. Ask a physician if you have concerns.

How do I find hidden moisture in my Fort Walton Beach home?

A moisture meter and a thermal camera. Our dispatch line can arrange a check.

Will opening windows help?

In humid weather it may add moisture. A dehumidifier in a closed room is usually better.

Is bleach a good fix?

No. It does not dry the material and can leave residue. Fix the moisture first.
