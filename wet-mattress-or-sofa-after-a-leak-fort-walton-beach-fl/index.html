Wet Mattress or Sofa After a Leak in Fort Walton Beach, FL

A soaked mattress or sofa is a common leftover after a leak. Whether to save it depends on the water, the material and how fast you act.

Call (844) 950-6900

By the Fort Walton Beach Water Damage Pros team

Furniture holds water in places you cannot see. Foam, padding and stuffing act like sponges. A cushion can feel dry on the surface while the core is wet. This guide explains what to try, what to skip and how to decide.

Start with the water type

Water sourceMattress or sofaWhy
Clean supply line or rain through roofMay be saved if dried fast.Low contamination.
Appliance or gray waterCase by caseSome contamination.
Sewage or floodwaterThrow outCannot be sanitized.
Water that sat for daysOften throw outOdor and mold risk.

Water categories follow the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard.

What to do right away

  • Move the item off the wet floor onto a dry surface or blocks.
  • Remove bedding, covers and removable cushions.
  • Blot with clean towels. Do not rub, which pushes water deeper.
  • Take photos for your insurance file before cleaning or tossing.
  • Use a wet-rated vacuum to pull water from upholstery if you have one.
  • Do not use a heater or hair dryer, which can scorch foam and trap moisture.

Mattresses: what works and what does not

Memory foam and innerspring mattresses both hold water deep inside. Dry the surface, then stand the mattress on its side in a dry room with a dehumidifier running. It can take days. If the mattress still smells musty, it is usually not worth keeping. Sleeping on a damp mattress invites mold.

Sofas and upholstered chairs

Wood frames can often be dried, but the padding may not recover. Remove cushions and stand them on edge. Use air movers and a dehumidifier. If fabric stains or smells persist, consider professional upholstery cleaning or replacement. Leather needs special care, because drying too fast cracks it.

The floor and walls matter more than the furniture

A wet sofa usually means wet carpet, pad or wall bottoms. Dry the structure first, or the furniture will get wet again. Read about water extraction and wall and drywall water damage repair for what to expect.

When to replace instead of save

  • The water was contaminated.
  • The item stayed wet for more than a day or two.
  • A musty smell remains after drying.
  • The cost to clean is close to replacement.
  • Your insurer will cover replacement and you have photos.

Other soft items: pillows, rugs, stuffed toys and clothes

ItemClean waterContaminated water
Pillows and stuffed toysLaunder if the label allows.Throw out.
Area rugsDry flat and clean.Discard or professional cleaning if valuable.
Clothing and linensWash and dry quickly.Discard or wash with sanitizer if safe.

When in doubt, throw it out if the water was contaminated.

Odor and what it tells you

A damp smell after drying usually means moisture is still inside the item. Sun and fresh air help on a dry day, but humid Gulf Coast air can work against you. Use a dehumidifier in a closed room instead. If the smell remains after several days, replacement is usually the better choice.

Can a professional dry and sanitize it?

Some upholstery cleaners and restoration companies can extract water from sofas and treat fabric. They can use hot water extraction, drying equipment and sanitizers. It works best when the water was clean and the cleaning is done within a day or two. Ask for a price before agreeing, and compare it to the cost of a replacement.

Protect the items you want to keep

  • Keep them away from heat sources that may scorch or warp them.
  • Keep them off bare wet floors.
  • Use plastic or foil blocks under legs on carpet.
  • Run a dehumidifier in a closed room.
  • Check the center of cushions before bringing them back.

Local note: after a major rain event

The National Weather Service called the April 29 and 30, 2014 rainfall in the Fort Walton Beach area a historic flash flood, and Okaloosa County declared a local state of emergency on April 30. After events like that, upholstered items that sat in outside water are usually discarded. Photograph them before you set them out for pickup, so your insurer can see what you lost.

Questions readers ask

Can a wet mattress be saved?

Sometimes, if the water was clean and you dry it quickly with airflow and a dehumidifier. If it smells musty after drying, replace it.

Is a sofa worth drying?

If the frame is solid and the water was clean, yes. Padding may need replacement.

Will insurance replace furniture?

It may if the water damage is covered. Take photos and ask your insurer.

Can I use a hair dryer?

No. It can damage foam and drive moisture deeper.

What should I do with furniture after a flood in Fort Walton Beach?

If it touched floodwater, plan to discard it. Photograph it first and check with your insurer.

How long can a mattress stay wet before it is ruined?

A day or two is a common line. Beyond that, odor and growth risks rise. Replace it if it smells musty after drying.

Can I put a wet cushion in the sun?

On a dry day, direct sun helps surface drying. Foam cores often stay damp, so check the center before you bring it back inside.
