If Water Is Still Spreading in Mary Esther

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve or the main shutoff. If you cannot find the valve, call the dispatcher while you look.

Protect what is dry

Lift rugs, boxes and electronics onto counters or beds before water reaches them.

Keep the power question simple

Document before you move

Take photos of the water line and damaged items. Your insurer will ask for them.

Older homes, slabs and storm water in Mary Esther

Many homes near Santa Rosa Sound sit on concrete slabs. A leak under the sink spreads across the slab and wicks into drywall at every wall it touches. After tropical weather, wind-driven rain also finds window sills and roof penetrations. Use the steps on storm water damage cleanup if the water came from outside.

Check your insurance policy for the difference between a sudden pipe break and a long-term leak. The first is often covered, and the second often is not.

A common Mary Esther scenario: the slow leak behind the cabinet

A typical call starts with a swollen cabinet base or a damp spot on the floor. The cause is often a slow drip from a supply hose or a drain trap. By the time the swelling shows, water has been moving into the subfloor and baseboard. Crews pull the toe kick, meter the wall and floor, and dry the area. If the cabinet base is particleboard, replacement is common.

Because Mary Esther runs its own water system, a street-level pressure drop is a different problem. Ask the city before you assume your plumbing is the cause. If you find water inside, see appliance leak cleanup or kitchen water damage restoration.
