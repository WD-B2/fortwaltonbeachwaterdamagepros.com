Emergency Appliance Leak Cleanup in Fort Walton Beach, FL

Washers, dishwashers, refrigerators and ice makers leak behind cabinets and under floors. Shut off the supply, then call to find and dry the hidden water.

24/7 dispatch line open

An appliance leak looks small until you lift the unit and find soaked flooring. Our dispatch line is open 24/7, and a call early in the leak keeps the damage small. Turn off the supply valve behind the appliance, unplug it only if the cord and floor are dry, and mop up what you can.

Quick steps when an appliance starts leaking

  • Shut the supply valve behind or beside the appliance. For a washer, close both hot and cold valves.
  • Unplug the unit only if you can reach the outlet without standing in water.
  • Pull the appliance out a little to see where water is coming from and where it traveled.
  • Blot standing water and place towels, but do not rely on towels to dry cabinets or subfloor.
  • Photograph the hose, connection, appliance label and damaged flooring.
  • Leave the appliance repair to a technician, and call us for water removal and drying.
  • If you smell gas, see sparks or hear buzzing near wet wiring, leave and call 911 or the utility before you call us.

Which appliances cause the most trouble

Washing machines

Supply hoses split or burst when under pressure. A drain hose popping off the standpipe sends water across the laundry floor.

Dishwashers

Door gaskets, inlet valves and drain connections leak slowly into the cabinet base and subfloor.

Refrigerators and ice makers

A small plastic supply line to the ice maker can split behind the unit and run for days before anyone sees it.

Air handlers and dehumidifiers

Full pans and clogged drain lines overflow onto closets and ceilings. See AC leak water damage repair.

What happens during an appliance leak cleanup

  1. Trace the water

    A meter and a thermal camera show how far water traveled under cabinets and beneath flooring.

  2. Extract and open up

    Standing water is removed. Toe kicks and baseboards come off, and wet materials are cut out when needed.

  3. Dry in place

    Air movers, mats or injection drying dry the subfloor and cabinets. Learn about this on the structural drying page.

  4. Confirm dry and document

    Final readings and photos go in your file for the repair and insurance conversation.

The longer a leak runs, the more material usually has to be replaced.

Check these appliance parts twice a year

  • Washer hoses: look for bulges, rust at the couplings and drips at the faucet.
  • Dishwasher door gasket and the supply line under the sink.
  • Refrigerator ice maker line and the water filter housing.
  • Water heater and softener connections.
  • Condensate lines from dehumidifiers and air conditioners.

A half hour twice a year catches most failures before they become a flooded floor.

What affects the cost of appliance leak cleanup

  • Duration of the leak and the amount of water released.
  • Flooring type under and around the appliance.
  • Whether water reached cabinets, walls or adjoining rooms.
  • Access to wet areas behind or beneath fixed appliances.
  • Number of drying days.
  • Rebuild items such as cabinets, flooring and paint.

Insurance note for appliance leaks

Sudden and accidental appliance failures are often covered, but long-running leaks may be excluded as maintenance issues. Keep the failed hose, take photos and ask your insurer how your policy reads.

If the leak affected a kitchen, see the kitchen water damage page for cabinet and countertop guidance.

Drying follows the ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage standard, which is the published reference for categories, classes and drying goals.

Questions we hear from Fort Walton Beach callers

Do you handle appliance leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Fort Walton Beach?

Yes. The dispatch line is open 24/7. Close the supply valve and call so a crew can extract water and dry cabinets and subfloor.

Can water under a dishwasher or fridge be dried without removing the appliance?

Sometimes. Crews can dry in place with injection drying or mats, but heavy damage may need the unit moved and flooring removed.

How do I know if a leak is hidden?

Look for a musty smell, swollen cabinet edges, lifted flooring or a floor that feels soft. A moisture meter confirms it.

Should I replace the hose or call a plumber?

A technician or plumber should replace damaged hoses. Braided stainless hoses are a common upgrade.

Does my insurer expect me to mitigate?

Most policies ask you to take reasonable steps to prevent further damage. Shutting off water, drying and keeping receipts are the usual steps.
