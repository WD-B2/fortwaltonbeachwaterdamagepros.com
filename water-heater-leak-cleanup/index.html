Emergency Water Heater Leak Cleanup in Fort Walton Beach, FL

A leaking water heater can flood a closet, garage or utility room. Cut power and water to the tank, then call for cleanup and drying.

Call (844) 950-6900
24/7 dispatch line open

A tank leak can start as a trickle and become a flood when the tank fails. Our dispatch line is open 24/7. Turn off power to the heater at the breaker or switch, close the cold water inlet valve and, for a gas unit, set the control to pilot or off before you call.

Shut down the water heater safely

  • Electric heater: switch off the breaker if the panel is dry. Gas heater: turn the gas control to off or pilot.
  • Close the cold water supply valve on the pipe above the tank.
  • Open a hot water tap in the house to relieve pressure and slow the drain.
  • Do not touch wet electrical parts. Stay out of standing water near the heater.
  • Attach a hose to the drain valve only if the area is safe and a drain is nearby.
  • Photograph the tank, label and leak location, and keep the failed part if a plumber removes it.
  • If you smell gas, see sparks or hear buzzing near wet wiring, leave and call 911 or the utility before you call us.

Why water heater leaks spread farther than you think

A 40 or 50 gallon tank holds enough water to cover a large floor area in a thin film. In homes where the heater sits in a garage, closet or attic, that water crosses the slab, seeps into walls and drains toward lower rooms. Attic installations are the worst case because water drops straight through the ceiling.

The cleanup process after a tank leak

  1. Confirm the unit is isolated

    The crew checks that water and power are off before any work starts.

  2. Extract and remove wet materials

    Pumps or extractors remove water. Wet drywall, baseboard and insulation at the base of walls are cut out when needed.

  3. Dry the room and adjoining walls

    Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the slab, framing and surrounding rooms. Learn more at structural drying and dehumidification.

  4. Document the loss

    Photos and meter logs support your insurance conversation and any plumber or warranty claim.

Warning signs before a tank fails

  • Rust-colored water from the hot tap.
  • Moisture or rust streaks at the base of the tank.
  • Rumbling or popping from sediment in the tank.
  • A tank that is older than the typical service life.
  • Water on the floor near the pressure relief valve discharge pipe.

Replacing a worn tank on your schedule is cheaper than replacing it after it floods a closet.

What affects the cost of water heater leak cleanup

  • How much water escaped and how long it ran.
  • Location of the heater: garage, closet, attic or utility room.
  • Flooring and wall materials touched by the water.
  • Whether the leak reached adjacent rooms.
  • Drying time and number of equipment pieces.
  • Rebuild items such as drywall, baseboard and paint.

Where your water heater sits changes the risk

A heater on a garage slab sends water toward the door and drain. A heater in a hall closet pushes water into carpet and drywall. An attic heater drops water onto insulation and the ceiling below, which is where ceiling water damage repair comes in. A drain pan with a line to the outside helps only if the line is clear.

Insurance note for water heater leaks

Sudden tank failure is often covered, but the heater itself and gradual leaks may not be. Check your policy, keep the old tank label and plumber invoice, and ask your insurer how the claim is handled.

Cleanup follows the ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage standard, and the crew records readings so your insurer and plumber have a clear record.

Questions we hear from Fort Walton Beach callers

Do you handle water heater emergencies at night or on weekends in Fort Walton Beach?

Yes. The 24/7 line accepts calls any hour. Shut off power and water to the heater first, then call so a crew can extract the water and begin drying.

Is water from a water heater clean?

It begins as clean supply water, but it can pick up sediment and contact with building materials. The crew checks the category and plans accordingly.

Should I drain the tank myself?

Only if you can do it safely with power and gas off and a drain nearby. Otherwise leave it for the plumber.

Will insurance pay for a failed water heater?

Policies differ. The heater itself is often excluded, while resulting water damage may be covered. Ask your insurer.

How do I tell if the leak is the tank or a fitting?

Check the connections and the relief valve first. Water coming from the tank body or jacket means the tank has failed.
