If Water Is Still Spreading in Seabreeze

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve or the main. In a condo, find out whether the building shutoff serves several units.

Contain it

Use towels and buckets to keep water from running to the floor below or into a neighbor's wall.

Keep clear of the power

Do not use wet outlets or appliances. Ask the dispatcher about the breaker.

Photograph and list

Photograph the source and the damage, and list what is wet, including furniture and electronics.

Drying and measuring

A crew extracts standing water, sets air movers and dehumidifiers and takes meter readings in walls and floors. The ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage standard is the published reference for categories and drying goals. Ask the crew to explain what the readings mean for your rooms.

In a condo, the crew may need to open a wall or ceiling shared with a neighbor. Management can arrange access. Water extraction explains the first stage.

Flood maps and storms in Seabreeze

We have not verified the FEMA flood zone for any street in Seabreeze. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Look up your own address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, and remember that a mapped zone describes risk on a map and does not predict whether your home will flood.

Being near the sound does not by itself prove a street floods, and we do not claim a specific storm flooded Seabreeze. If outside water enters, treat it as contaminated, stay out and call for professional help.

Insurance and condo documents

A condo master policy and your own policy may each cover different parts of a loss. Ask your insurer and your association what applies. Keep photos, readings and every message with management.
