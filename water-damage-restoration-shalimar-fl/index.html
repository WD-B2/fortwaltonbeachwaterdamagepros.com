If Water Is Still Spreading in Shalimar

Stop the source

Close the nearest shutoff, then the main. Open a faucet at the lowest point to drain the line.

Lift and move

Raise furniture legs onto blocks and move rugs and boxes off the floor.

Power safety

Photograph

A common Shalimar scenario: rain driven through a bay-facing wall

Homes near Choctawhatchee Bay are exposed to wind from open water. A strong storm can push rain sideways through window frames, wall vents and under siding. The water runs down inside the wall and soaks the bottom plate and insulation. Stains may show only at baseboards days later.

Crews check the wall with a meter, open small sections to see the cavity and set drying equipment. The roofer or window contractor repairs the entry point. Read about wall and drywall repair for what to expect in the wall itself.

Checking your bay-side property after a storm

After a storm, walk the property. Look at the foundation line, door thresholds, window sills and the garage floor. Photograph any water marks. Check the attic if it is safe. Wind-driven rain can wet insulation that dries on the surface within days, so a meter check is useful even if nothing looks wrong.
