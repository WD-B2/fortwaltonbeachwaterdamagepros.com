Homes, rentals and condos in one area

Listings around Alder Avenue and Magnolia Avenue include small apartment buildings with one-bedroom and two-bedroom units for rent. Listings along Brooks Street SE show condominium units. Single-family homes sit among them.

That mix changes the first call. A tenant should tell the landlord or property manager right away. A condo owner should tell building management, because water can run into the unit below. A homeowner calls the dispatch line directly and then the insurer.

If Water Is Still Spreading in Elliott Point

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve, then the unit or building shutoff. In a multi-unit building, tell the manager at once.

Protect the unit below

Contain what you can with towels and buckets so water does not drip through the ceiling below.

Power safety

Stay away from wet outlets and fixtures. Do not use electrical equipment standing in water.

Write it down

Note the time, the source and who you told. Photograph the damage before cleanup.

Drying a unit or a house

The crew extracts the water, then dries walls and floors with air movers and dehumidifiers and tracks the readings. The ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage standard is the reference for categories, classes and drying goals. Ask which category the water fell into and why.

Shared walls and ceilings need care. The crew may need access to neighboring units to check for moisture, so tell the manager early. Structural drying explains how readings guide the work.

Flood maps and storms in Elliott Point

Look up your own address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, and remember that a mapped zone describes risk on a map and does not predict whether your home will flood.

An area near the water is not the same as an area that floods, and we do not claim a particular storm flooded Elliott Point. If water does come in from outside, treat it as contaminated, keep out and call. Storm water damage cleanup covers wind and rain losses.

Insurance, landlords and records
